Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply.

What are the promo rates?
Weekdays are ₱17,000 per night and weekends are ₱20,000 per night. These are limited-time promo rates.
How many guests can stay?
The villa fits up to 16 guests across 8 beds in 4 rooms. A maximum of 20 guests can stay using extra beds. For 17 to 20 guests, message the host to confirm the rate.
Do promo rates apply during holidays?
No. Promo rates exclude peak seasons and holidays. Ask the host for the price of those dates.
How do I get there?
The villa is in Amaras Farm, Barangay Sinaliw, Malaki, Alfonso. Use the Google Maps button in the location section for directions.
How can I check availability?
Open our Airbnb calendar to see open dates and current rates. You can also send us your preferred dates directly.
How do I book?
Send your dates and number of guests through Airbnb or Facebook. The host confirms availability before you pay.
